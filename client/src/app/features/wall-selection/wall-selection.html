Tips & shortcuts
  • Polygon (P): click corners; click the first point or press Enter to finish.
  • Adjust: with the polygon tool, drag any corner point.
  • Brush (B) / Eraser (E): paint to add or remove areas such as windows. [ ] change size.
  • Move (V): drag to pan, click a wall to select it. Hold Space to pan with any tool.
  • Zoom: scroll, pinch, or + / −; 0 fits the photo.
  • Undo: Ctrl+Z, redo with Ctrl+Shift+Z.
