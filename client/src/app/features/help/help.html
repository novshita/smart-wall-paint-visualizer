How to use

Preview paint on a photo of your own room in four steps. It takes about five minutes the first time.

  1. add_photo_alternate

    1. Upload a photo

    From your dashboard, choose Upload a photo. Use a JPG or PNG of a room you own or have permission to use. Location and camera details are removed when it's saved, and only you can see it.

  2. polyline

    2. Select the walls

    Use the polygon tool to click each corner of a wall, or the brush to paint over it. Then use the eraser on windows, doors, pictures and furniture. Add one wall per surface you want to colour separately.

  3. format_paint

    3. Paint and compare

    Pick a wall, then a colour from the library, your favourites, recent colours or your own HEX code. Choose Solid, Two-tone or Pattern, and adjust finish, opacity and brightness. Use Compare to slide between before and after, and + to try a second idea.

  4. download

    4. Save and download

    Changes save automatically. Click Save to add the design to My designs, and Download a full-size image or a before-and-after with the colour names and codes.

Tips for realistic results

  • Take photos in even daylight; avoid flash and strong sun patches.
  • Stand back so the whole wall is in view, and hold the camera level.
  • Zoom in when tracing corners; a few extra points make edges much cleaner.
  • Use a little edge softness (2–4 px) where walls meet ceilings.
  • Always check a real sample pot on the wall before buying paint.

Questions

Why does the colour look different from the paint card?

Screens, room lighting and wall texture all change how a colour looks. The preview keeps the light and shadow from your photo, but it is still a guide. Test a sample on your wall before deciding.

Who can see my photos?

Only you. Photos are stored privately and shown through links that expire. Deleting a design deletes its photo permanently.

Can I change a wall after painting it?

Yes. Go back to Select walls: your selections stay editable. Changes to a wall's shape apply to every design variant.

Can I use it on a tablet?

Yes. Pinch to zoom, drag with two fingers to pan, and tap to place polygon points.
